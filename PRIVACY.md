# Anchor privacy policy

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

Anchor is a browser extension that keeps web video and audio paused until you resume it. This policy explains what Anchor does with information. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

## What Anchor accesses

To find video and audio players, Anchor runs on the HTTP and HTTPS pages you visit, except sites on your ignored-sites list. It looks only at media elements and player controls on the page so it can hold a pause and handle keyboard and media keys. It does not read, save or send page content, browsing history, form data or anything you type.

## What Anchor stores

- **Your settings and ignored-sites list** are kept in your browser's local extension storage on your device.
- **Pause holds** (which tab and player are being kept paused) are kept in session storage and are deleted when the tab closes or the browser restarts.

Removing the extension deletes all of this.

## What Anchor does not do

- It makes no network requests. There are no servers, accounts, analytics, telemetry, ads or tracking.
- It does not load or run remote code. Everything it runs ships in the extension.
- It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone, because it does not collect any.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
