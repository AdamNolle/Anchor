Anchor · v1.3.0

Paused should mean paused.

Anchor automatically detects web video and audio players on HTTP and HTTPS sites, including your Jellyfin server and players embedded from other hosts. A deliberate pause stays held until you choose Play, and only for the player you paused. Other videos, the next episode, and new titles play normally. Sites you ignore are left completely alone.

Install or update in Brave, Chrome, Edge, or another Chromium browser

  1. Extract Anchor.zip or the repository to a permanent folder.
  2. Open brave://extensions, chrome://extensions, or edge://extensions. Enable Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the folder containing manifest.json. To update an existing installation from this folder, click its Reload button.
  4. Approve website access if the browser asks; automatic detection across sites and iframe hosts needs HTTP/HTTPS access.
  5. Pin Anchor using the browser's extension menu. Open tabs are protected right away; reload a page for the strongest protection of players it already created.

What the icon means

White on black: an enabled player with a media source is detected. Gray: the site is ignored, no playable media is detected, the media source failed, or this page cannot host extensions. A black icon confirms detection, not a test of a service's entire player implementation.

Ignored sites

Anchor does not run on ignored sites at all. To ignore the current site, turn off Protect this site in the popup. To add or remove any site, open Ignored sites & settings from the popup (or the extension's Options). An entry such as example.com also covers www.example.com and other subdomains. You can enter a hostname, a full web address, or an IP address.

Social feeds (X/Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, Reddit, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Snapchat, Tumblr, Bluesky) are ignored by default. Their videos autoplay and pause as you scroll, and they use Space and K for their own shortcuts. Remove an entry to protect that site.

Playback and media keys

  • Pause with a recognized player control or Space / K. Play, Resume, Next episode, or choosing another episode releases the hold.
  • A pause holds that player, including a replacement in the same spot (a source change or ad transition). Other videos on the page, such as a news article's other clips or the rest of a feed, keep playing.
  • Opening a different title releases the hold, so the next video autoplays, including in-app navigation such as Hulu's or YouTube's. Reloading the same page keeps it.
  • Keep paused in the popup holds every player in the tab, including players that load afterward. Resume releases the hold and starts the main detected player.
  • Use hardware Play/Pause, Play, Pause, or Stop. Stop holds the video without resetting its position. Media keys also work while typing.
  • Anchor registers fallback Media Session play/pause/stop handlers for players that have none. It preserves the site's own handlers and restores them when disabled. The OS or browser chooses which app or tab receives hardware media actions.
  • Optional quick toggle: assign your own shortcut at brave://extensions/shortcuts, chrome://extensions/shortcuts, or edge://extensions/shortcuts.
  • Space / K apply to a player you are watching: a large unmuted video, or one you have used. Small or muted autoplay clips never take over Space, so pages keep scrolling. ← / → seek 10 seconds only on players without their own arrow shortcuts. Keys are left alone in text fields, sliders, menus, focused buttons and links.

Optional preferences

  • Stop autoplay & next episodes: each video waits until you start it, and the next episode waits when one ends. This may also stop ad-to-content transitions.
  • Lock every pause: catches unrecognized and programmatic pauses. It can interrupt buffering or source transitions.
  • Pause when leaving this tab: holds playing media when the tab is hidden. Returning does not automatically resume.

Troubleshooting

If an unfamiliar pause control is not held, use Keep paused and Resume, or enable Lock every pause. If a site misbehaves with Anchor, ignore it from the popup.

After reloading, the browser may require clicking the site's Play button once before popup Resume can work. If media keys control another tab or app, make your streaming tab the active media session by playing it once. OS media routing and apps that reserve hardware keys remain outside the extension's control. Turn off keyboard controls to keep a site's own Space/K shortcuts.

Privacy and limits

Website access is used only for local media detection and control. Preferences and session pause state remain in the browser. Anchor has no telemetry, server, network requests, remote code, or browsing-history log. Session records are deleted when a tab closes.

Anchor supports standard HTML video/audio players and does not repair server outages, network buffering, unsupported codecs, DRM failures, or native apps. It does not skip ads or bypass DRM. Platform updates and unusual custom controls may need adjustments.

A hold is retained across reloads of the same page during a browser session. It clears when you explicitly play, open a different title or site, ignore the site, close the tab, or restart the browser.
